## Selected SAP Ariba projects

### Contract Compliance, Catalog & PO Validation
Built and validated a 3-level contract hierarchy with supplier, commodity and item-level pricing controls.

**Results:** 2% supplier discount · 3% / 4% / 5% commodity tiers · USD 47.50 / USD 45.00 quantity breaks · USD 6,750 requisition → PO · Auto-Catalog USD 15.00 → USD 14.70.

Repository: https://github.com/eshwarreddy24/SAP-Ariba-Contract-Compliance-Catalog-PO

### Service Contract, Receiving & Lifecycle Controls
Built a no-release standalone service contract with receiving, milestone and lifecycle controls.

**Results:** USD 500K ceiling · 3% tolerance · USD 250/hour · 1,000-hour limit · 60-hour service receipt = USD 15,000 · USD 10K milestone · USD 10K minimum-commitment amendment.

Repository: https://github.com/eshwarreddy24/SAP-Ariba-Service-Contract-Receiving-Lifecycle
